The Germany Opportunity Card lets qualified workers move to Germany to look
for a job.

Always confirm fees, processing times and eligibility on the official German
government sources before quoting them. Never promise approval. Ask for the
caller's qualifications, language level and passport country, then offer a
callback with the current document list.
